## Rules (to stay within Apple's guidelines)

- Only ask people who have **actually used** the app.
- Ask for an **honest** rating, never a 5-star one specifically.
- **No rewards** of any kind for ratings: no gifts, discounts or free Premium.
- Don't ask close family to rate it just to help. Apple treats that as manipulation.
- Ratings are per country. Most search traffic is in the **US**, so ratings from US users help most. Canadian ratings only show in the Canadian store.
- The app now asks for a rating by itself after a child's first completed daily goal (from version 1.2).
