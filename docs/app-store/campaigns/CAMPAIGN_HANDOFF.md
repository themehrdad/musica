## Goal and product

Mehrdad's goal is greater relevant App Store visibility, more downloads, and ultimately more subscriptions. Raw impressions and cheap installs are intermediate measures; the app needs users who actually have access to a piano.

Musica shows an individual note on a staff, listens through the iPhone microphone as the learner plays a real piano, and gives feedback, hints and celebrations. It is designed for children and family practice alongside lessons. An acoustic piano or a digital piano with speakers on is required. It is not a streaming player, touchscreen piano, piano-tiles game, or full-song course.

The version 1.1 release package provides 20 correct notes per day free, one learner profile and treble-clef practice. Premium includes unlimited practice, additional profiles, bass/grand staff and the progress calendar/history. Purchases are behind a parental gate. Monthly/yearly subscriptions and an eligible-subscriber seven-day trial were retained, with Family Sharing described in the listing. Check live localized pricing and eligibility before quoting an offer; old listing prices are not a current Canadian price check.
